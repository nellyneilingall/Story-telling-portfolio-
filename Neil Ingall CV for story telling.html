About

A Creative
with Range

I've worked in arts, music, film, and TV for over 20 years — delivering creative projects of all sizes, from large commercial works to smaller passion projects. I'm equally comfortable developing ideas from inception to completion, or joining a production mid-flow.

Through years embedded in wheeled sports communities across London, Europe, and North America, I've built connections across a rich tapestry of subcultures: contemporary arts, photography, fashion, LGBTQI+ communities, skate culture. These communities shape how I see the world — and what I want to make.

My current focus is on developing ideas that can inspire positive change and raise awareness.
